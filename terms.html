Feelturn: Terms of Use

Version 1, November 10, 2026

Theory, not training

The lessons explain technique with simplified 3D models. The app doesn't train your body or build riding skills, doesn't see you, doesn't measure your movements and doesn't rate your skill. Finishing a lesson doesn't mean you can do the move safely on snow.

The game is a game

Its physics and courses are invented. Real snow, ice and your own body behave differently, so your results in the game say nothing about what you can do on a slope. Don't copy its speeds, jumps, spins or flips on a real slope.

No substitute for an instructor

Learn new moves on snow with a qualified instructor, on a slope that suits your level. Wear a helmet and protective gear.

Snowboarding is risky

Falls and collisions can cause serious injury or death, even with good technique. You ride at your own risk, follow the slope rules and decide for yourself what you are ready for. If you have any health concerns, ask a doctor first.

Only when you're stopped

Don't use the app while riding, on a lift, or anywhere it takes your attention from what's around you.

Liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, the developer is not liable for any injury, death, damage or loss connected with snowboarding or with acting on the app's content. This doesn't limit any rights that the law of your country does not allow to be limited.

If you don't accept them, please don't use the app.
