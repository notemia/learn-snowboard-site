Feelturn: Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short The app does not collect personal data. Everything you mark and write stays on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app works offline.

What the app stores on your device

Your chosen language, stance and experience, your haptics, sound and control settings, lessons completed in the app, your observations, saved cues, personal notes and best game scores. The app never sends this data anywhere. It may be included in your iPhone backup if you have backups turned on.

Motion sensors and camera

In the lessons, if you turn on tilt control, and during rides in the game, the app reads the phone’s tilt and movement to steer the board on screen. These values are not stored or sent anywhere. The camera and microphone are not used: the game’s sounds are generated inside the app. Location and Health data are not used.

Purchase

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details, and the app only learns whether the full version has been bought.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. The app has no third-party SDKs, ads or analytics. It makes no network requests of its own.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset practice history removes your progress or all practice data. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Accepting the terms of use

When you accept the terms of use, the app stores on your device which version of the terms you accepted and when, so it can ask again if the terms change. This record never leaves the device. The terms are on this site’s Terms of use page and in the app under Settings → Terms of use.

Children

The app is made for adult beginners and does not collect data about anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
