Where do I start?

Open the Lessons tab and start with level one: the rules on the slope and how to fall and get up. The levels suggest an order.

How do I steer the ball by tilting the phone?

In the turn lessons choose “Ride it yourself”, turn on “Control with phone tilt” and press Start. Hold the phone flat like a small board: tip the top away from you for toes, toward you for heels. You can also steer with a finger.

How does the purchase work?

The app is a one-time purchase with no subscription. Details are on its App Store page.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new iPhone with the same Apple Account, open Settings → Restore purchase.

How do I control the game?

Tilt left or right to put the board on its edge. Tip the top away for more speed. A finger on the screen squats, and letting go jumps. In the air, tilt spins and flips, and a finger grabs. In detail: Game tab → How to control.

The board drifts to one side. What can I do?

The phone’s position during the countdown becomes neutral. Pause, hold the phone comfortably and tap Continue: the countdown sets it again. If tilt doesn’t suit you, turn it off in the Game tab’s settings and steer with a finger.

How do I turn off the sound?

The iPhone’s silent mode mutes the game’s sounds, and Game tab → Wind and edge sounds turns them off. Your music keeps playing.

How do I find my stance?

The app asks which foot leads. If you’re not sure, choose “I don’t know yet” and it shows an example stance, clearly labeled. You can change it in Settings. Your writing hand doesn’t decide your stance.

Does the app rate my riding?

No. The scenes are simplified teaching models. The app doesn’t use the camera and can’t see how you ride. Finishing a lesson in the app doesn’t mean you can do the move safely on snow.

Does the app replace an instructor? Who is responsible if I get hurt?

No. The lessons are theory on simplified models, and the game is a game with invented physics. Learn new moves on snow with an instructor, and don’t copy the game’s jumps or speeds. You ride at your own risk; the full terms of use are on the Terms of use page and in the app under Settings → Terms of use.

Do I need the internet?

No. All content is built into the app, and no account is needed.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language: system default or one of nine languages. It applies immediately.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset practice history. You can reset progress only, or everything.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
