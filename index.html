Snowboarding from scratchUnderstand the moves before your first day on the slope

Watching and reading help you understand technique. Here you also control the rider yourself: you try a move, see the result and can change something. Motor-skill training uses this same loop: action, feedback, another try. It helps you take a move apart; doing it with your body is something you learn on the snow.

Shift your weight, change edges, bend your knees and see right away how the board reacts: where it turned, how speed and direction changed. You can slow the move down, try it different ways and compare the result. The details get clearer: what to do, why, and how the body moves and the board reacts.

Put your experience in order and spot your mistakes: wrong moves harden into habits and we stop noticing them. Get to grips with linked turns and the basics of carving, and ride the simulator along the way.
